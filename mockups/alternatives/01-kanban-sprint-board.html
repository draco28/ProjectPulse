Option 1: Kanban Sprint Board

✓ Advantages

  • • Tickets are the ONLY unit of work (no phases/weeks/days)
  • • Progress derived automatically from ticket status
  • • Simple mental model: move tickets left → right
  • • Agent can claim "In Progress" ticket directly
  • • No "current position" confusion - just "current sprint"
  • • Familiar UX (Jira, Linear, Trello pattern)

✗ Disadvantages

  • • No timeline visualization
  • • Sprint boundaries might feel arbitrary
  • • Hard to see long-term roadmap/phases
  • • Doesn't show dependencies between tickets
Key Change: DELETE Phase, Week, Day models. Keep Sprint + Ticket only. Sprint.number is the grouping key.
