Why Nested Cards?

Solves clutter by hiding child tasks inside parent features by default. Click to expand and see all subtasks inline without leaving the board view.

  • • Compact by default - See feature-level progress at a glance
  • • Expand on demand - Drill into tasks when needed
  • • Progress rolls up - Feature shows % based on child completion
  • • Active tasks highlighted - Yellow ring on currently worked items
