Option 2: Milestone Timeline

✓ Advantages

  • • Clear deliverable focus (what ships, not when you're busy)
  • • Timeline visualization without day-level noise
  • • Milestones = natural demo/release points
  • • Tickets link to milestones, not abstract time units
  • • Progress = deliverables complete (not arbitrary %)
  • • Easy to see "where are we in the big picture"

✗ Disadvantages

  • • Milestones can be vague if not well-defined
  • • Doesn't show sprint velocity or time allocation
  • • Requires upfront planning of deliverables
  • • May not fit agile "evolving scope" well
Key Change: Replace Phase/Sprint/Week/Day with Milestone model. Ticket.milestoneId instead of scheduledWeekId. Milestones have targetDate, no duration tracking.
